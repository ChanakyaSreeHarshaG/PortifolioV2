The 8 Essential Live-Coding Patterns

90% of live coding SQL interview questions boil down to these fundamental architectural patterns. Recognize the wording, and the solution structure follows immediately:

PatternHow it sounds in interviewCanonical Solution Template
Nth Highest Value "Find 2nd/3rd highest salary", "Top score" DENSE_RANK() OVER (ORDER BY col DESC) = N
Top-N Per Group "Top 3 earners per department", "Best seller in region" ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY dept ORDER BY score DESC) <= N
Finding Duplicates "Identify duplicate emails / customer rows" GROUP BY key_col HAVING COUNT(*) > 1
Deleting Duplicates "Keep the earliest ID and delete remainder" ROW_NUMBER() OVER (PARTITION BY key ORDER BY id); DELETE rn > 1
Running Total / Moving Avg "Cumulative revenue", "7-day rolling average" SUM(amt) OVER (ORDER BY date ROWS BETWEEN UNBOUNDED PRECEDING AND CURRENT ROW)
Gaps & Islands "3+ consecutive login days", "Missing IDs in sequence" date - CAST(ROW_NUMBER() OVER (...) AS INT) AS grp
Row-Over-Row Change "Month-over-month growth %", "Price change" LAG(revenue, 1) OVER (ORDER BY month)
Cohorts & Sessions "Customer retention by signup month", "Sessionize clicks" LAG() gap check + running SUM() of session change flags
